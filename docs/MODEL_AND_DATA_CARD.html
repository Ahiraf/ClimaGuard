No models were trained or fine-tuned from scratch. All models are used as pre-trained foundation models via official hosted APIs, called server-side only. API usage costs are borne by the team.

Known Limitations & Ethical Considerations

  • Not medical advice. ClimaGuard is a humanitarian first-response aid, not a diagnosis tool; this is stated in-product. Users are directed to professional care and local emergency services.
  • AI uncertainty. Guidance and single-photo vision triage can be wrong; outputs are framed as first-response suggestions, not clinical conclusions.
  • Language variance. Output quality depends on the foundation model’s strength per language; low-resource languages may be less reliable.
  • Privacy. Anonymous authentication is used; no user names or accounts are required. Reports are minimized to what powers guidance and the aggregate heatmap.
  • Bias & safety. Prompts use humanitarian, age-appropriate framing; the vision route detects and handles model safety refusals rather than failing silently.
  • Data currency. Live features require connectivity; offline mode preserves the last guidance only.
