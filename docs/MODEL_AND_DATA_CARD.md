# ClimaGuard — Model & Data Card

**SciBlitz AI Challenge 2026 · Track B** · One-page summary of AI models and data sources.

## Models Used

| Model | Provider | Role | License / Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gemini 2.5 Flash** | Google | Primary model — text generation, function calling, multi-turn chat, multimodal vision, audio transcription | Google Gemini API Terms of Service (commercial API, pre-trained) |
| **GPT-4o** | OpenAI | Fallback for text & vision when Gemini is rate-limited/unavailable | OpenAI API Terms of Service (commercial API, pre-trained) |
| **Whisper** | OpenAI | Fallback for audio transcription | OpenAI API Terms of Service (commercial API, pre-trained) |

No models were trained or fine-tuned from scratch. All models are used as **pre-trained foundation
models via official hosted APIs**, called server-side only. API usage costs are borne by the team.

## Data Sources

| Data | Source | Use | License / Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live weather & air quality | **Open-Meteo API** | Real-time hazard input to risk assessment | Free, no key; CC-BY 4.0 attribution |
| Country → language & coordinates | Internal (`lib/languages.ts`), BCP-47 standard | Localization for 25 countries | Project-authored |
| Emergency helpline numbers | Internal (`lib/emergencyContacts.ts`), public official sources | Tap-to-call in each country | Public information |
| User reports (child age, country, symptoms) | Generated by app users | Age-specific guidance; anonymized aggregate heatmap | Stored under anonymous Firebase auth; no PII names required |
| Problem framing | *UNICEF Children's Climate Risk Report* | Motivation and country risk selection | Cited; not redistributed |

No third-party training datasets are used — the system relies on foundation-model reasoning over
live API data, not on a trained dataset.

## Known Limitations & Ethical Considerations

- **Not medical advice.** ClimaGuard is a humanitarian first-response aid, not a diagnosis tool;
  this is stated in-product. Users are directed to professional care and local emergency services.
- **AI uncertainty.** Guidance and single-photo vision triage can be wrong; outputs are framed as
  first-response suggestions, not clinical conclusions.
- **Language variance.** Output quality depends on the foundation model's strength per language;
  low-resource languages may be less reliable.
- **Privacy.** Anonymous authentication is used; no user names or accounts are required. Reports are
  minimized to what powers guidance and the aggregate heatmap.
- **Bias & safety.** Prompts use humanitarian, age-appropriate framing; the vision route detects and
  handles model safety refusals rather than failing silently.
- **Data currency.** Live features require connectivity; offline mode preserves the last guidance
  only.
